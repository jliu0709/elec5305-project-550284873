# Comparative Evaluation of Spectral Subtraction and Wiener Filtering for Speech Enhancement under Different Noise Conditions

This ELEC5305 project compares spectral subtraction and Wiener filtering for speech enhancement under different noise conditions. The project will be implemented in MATLAB and evaluated using speech signals mixed with white noise and office noise at different signal-to-noise ratios.

## Methods

- Spectral subtraction
- Wiener filtering
- Short-time Fourier transform
- Overlap-add reconstruction

## Data

- Mini LibriSpeech clean speech
- MATLAB-generated white noise
- DEMAND office noise
